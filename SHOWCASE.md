## ⚡ The Story Behind A.R.E.S.

Most open-source desktop assistants are built around brittle `if "keyword" in query:` scripts and vintage robotic text-to-speech synthesizers. When you ask them a natural question, they break. Furthermore, cloud-based assistants continuously transmit personal queries, source code, and telemetry to third-party data centers.

**A.R.E.S.** was engineered to solve both problems:
1. **Air-Gapped Cognitive Brain**: Powered by local LLMs via **Ollama** (Llama 3.2, DeepSeek-R1, Qwen 2.5), running entirely on local unified memory without sending a single byte to external clouds.
2. **Hyper-Realistic Neural Voice**: Pipelined streaming voice synthesis via **Edge-TTS** with native hardware acceleration (`afplay` on macOS), creating a human-like, authoritative voice experience.
3. **Autonomous Action Dispatcher**: The AI seamlessly translates natural language intents into real operating system actions (retina screenshots, zero-key weather lookups, Spotify/Music controls, and deep hardware telemetry).
4. **Futuristic Terminal HUD**: Styled with Rich terminal tables, glowing status indicators, and cyberpunk ASCII aesthetics.

---

## 🛠️ Feature Matrix

- 🎙️ **Neural Voice Output**: Low-latency, human-like voice synthesis with non-blocking sentence pipelining.
- 📸 **Retina Screenshot Capture**: Instant macOS desktop capture.
- 🌦️ **Zero-Key Live Weather**: Real-time meteorological intelligence for any city worldwide.
- 🎵 **Spotify & Apple Music Controls**: Play, pause, and skip tracks via AppleScript.
- 🖥️ **Host Telemetry**: Live CPU %, RAM MB, battery health, and disk availability.
- 🛡️ **Air-Gapped Privacy Shield**: Zero telemetry, zero tracking.
